About This Document

What this is
An English-language reference compendium of every exercise catalogued on the training website of Coach Pedro Miguel, a Portuguese karate and physical-preparation coach. It gathers 544 exercises spanning physical capacities, performance, movement preparation, general training and karate technique — consolidated into 442 entries, with closely related variants grouped under a single heading. It is a companion to the full local site mirror (index.html) held in the same folder.
Where it came from
The source is the coach's own website, sites.google.com/view/coachpedromiguel — a Portuguese-language Google Sites site organised entirely around demonstration videos. It was accessed and reproduced with the owner's consent, for personal study. This document keeps the site's original section structure.
How we got here
The full 57-page site was crawled and mirrored locally. Because every exercise on the site is presented as a video, the video captions — not the surrounding theory text — were used as the definitive exercise list: an earlier text-only extraction was discarded because, in plain text, exercise names and theory headings are indistinguishable. The site was then re-scanned video-by-video to produce an accurate list, each name was translated from Portuguese to English, and, where the movement could be identified, elaborated with a short note on execution and training purpose.
Reading the elaborations
Strength, conditioning and standard physical-preparation movements carry full elaboration. Karate techniques include translation of the Japanese terms. The coach's own bespoke drills (cone, hoop, foam-roller and partner work) are translated with a functional note on the quality they develop — inferred from the exercise name, so worth checking against the original videos as the compendium is edited. Pages that contain only theory (e.g. LTAD, Motor Skills, Energy Systems) are listed but flagged as having no catalogued exercises.
